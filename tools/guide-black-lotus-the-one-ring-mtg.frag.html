The most expensive Magic: The Gathering card ever sold is a Black Lotus from the 1993 Alpha set, graded CGC Pristine 10, which sold privately for $3,000,000 in April 2024. Guinness World Records lists it as the most expensive Magic card sold in a private sale. In second place is The One Ring 001/001, the only copy of its kind, which the rapper Post Malone bought for a reported $2 million in 2023.

Here is what each card is, why they cost so much, and how their records compare.

What is Black Lotus?

Black Lotus is one of the cards from Magic's very first set, Limited Edition Alpha, released in August 1993. It costs nothing to play: you sacrifice it to add three mana of any one colour, which lets you cast big spells turns earlier than you should. That makes it one of the most powerful cards ever printed. It belongs to the "Power Nine", nine early cards so strong that they are banned in almost every format; in Vintage you may play a single copy.

Black Lotus was printed three times, all in 1993: Alpha and Beta with black borders, and Unlimited with white borders. The original art is by Christopher Rush. It is on Wizards of the Coast's Reserved List, a promise never to reprint certain early cards in tournament-legal form, so no new copies will ever be printed. That is a big part of why prices keep climbing.

Black Lotus price records

In 2013 a good copy sold on eBay for about $27,000. By 2021 a signed PSA 10 Alpha copy sold for $511,100, and in 2023 a signed artist proof sold for $615,000 at Heritage. Post Malone also owns a signed Christopher Rush artist proof, which he reportedly paid more than $800,000 for.

The record came in April 2024, when the only Alpha Black Lotus graded CGC Pristine 10 sold privately for $3 million, as confirmed by CGC Cards. The public auction record followed in September 2026, when a PSA 10 Alpha copy, one of only three, sold for $2,208,200 at Goldin.

How to tell the printings apart: Alpha and Beta both have black borders, but Alpha cards have more rounded corners. Unlimited has a white border. Alpha copies are worth the most, then Beta, then Unlimited.

What is The One Ring 001/001?

The One Ring is a legendary artifact from The Lord of the Rings: Tales of Middle-earth, the Magic set released on June 23, 2023. Most copies are ordinary cards. But Wizards of the Coast printed exactly one special version, numbered 001/001, with its text written in the Black Speech of Mordor, and hid it in a single Collector Booster pack.

The hunt made headlines around the world, and a $1 million bounty was offered for it before it was found. In June 2023 a collector in Toronto, Brook Trafton, opened it; PSA confirmed the card and graded it Mint 9. In August 2023 he sold it to Post Malone, a long-time Magic player, for a reported $2 million. It was the first Magic card to sell for over $1 million. Post Malone still owns it, and in August 2026 he took it out of its case and played it in a game on The Command Zone's Game Knights show.

Black Lotus is valuable because it is old, powerful and can never be reprinted, and there are thousands of copies at different prices. The One Ring 001/001 is valuable because there is only one. The regular One Ring is an ordinary card that players use in decks.

Quick answers

What is the most expensive Magic: The Gathering card? An Alpha Black Lotus graded CGC Pristine 10, sold privately for $3 million in April 2024. The auction record is $2,208,200 for a PSA 10 Alpha Black Lotus at Goldin in September 2026.

How much is a Black Lotus worth? It depends hugely on the printing and condition: Unlimited copies are the cheapest, Alpha the most expensive, and top-graded Alpha copies sell for millions. Check recent sold prices for the exact printing and grade.

Will Black Lotus be reprinted? Not as a tournament-legal card. It is on the Reserved List.

Who owns The One Ring 001/001? Post Malone, who bought it from Brook Trafton in August 2023 for a reported $2 million.

How many copies of The One Ring 001/001 exist? One. Other versions of The One Ring were printed in normal numbers.

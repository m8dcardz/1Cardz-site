What is a One Piece Card Game starter deck?

A starter deck is a ready-to-play deck built around one Leader card. Open it and you can play a full game under the official rules straight away: one Leader, a 50-card main deck of Characters, Events and Stages, and DON!! cards, the resource you spend to play cards and power up attacks. Most regular starter decks have an MSRP of US $11.99. The bigger "Ultra" and "EX" decks cost more and usually come with extra foil cards or a second Leader.

Because each deck is a single, focused strategy, starter decks are the easiest way to learn the One Piece Card Game, and many of their cards still turn up in tournament decks.

Learning with a friend or a child: the SD-01 Set Sail Deck Set (September 18, 2026, MSRP $24.99) is made for exactly this: two decks in one box, so two people can sit down and play.

Learning on your own: pick one of the six 2026 starter decks, ST-31 to ST-36 (July 31, 2026, $11.99 each). Each is one colour and one Leader, which keeps the game simple while you learn. Start with the character you like most; you'll play it more.

A note on Standard play

Since April 1, 2026, the One Piece Card Game has a rotating Standard format built on block icons printed on each card. The oldest block, which covers the first four booster sets and the earliest starter decks, has left Standard and is now played in the all-cards "Extra Regulation" format. If you want to play at store tournaments, choose a recent deck. Older decks are still fine for casual games at home.

Quick answers

How many One Piece starter decks are there? 38 numbered decks (ST-01 to ST-38, with ST-37 and ST-38 out December 18, 2026), plus two multi-deck sets.

What's the best One Piece starter deck for beginners? For two players, the SD-01 Set Sail Deck Set. For one player, any of the 2026 single-colour decks, ST-31 to ST-36.

How much is a One Piece starter deck? Most have an MSRP of US $11.99. EX, Ultra and special decks run from $16.99 to $34.99.

Can I play old starter decks in tournaments? The oldest cards have rotated out of Standard since April 2026, but they're still legal in Extra Regulation and fine for casual play.

What is a Disney Lorcana starter deck?

A Disney Lorcana starter deck is a ready-to-play 60-card deck built from two of the game's six inks, so you can open it and play a full game straight away. From 2023 to 2025 every set came with its own starter decks, 21 in all; the first ones launched at US $16.99 each. In 2026 Ravensburger switched to 2-Player Starter Sets, which put two decks in one box for $29.99.

A classic starter deck holds the 60-card deck, including two foil cards of the characters on the box, plus a 12-card booster pack, damage counters and a rulebook. Later decks added a paper playmat and a lore tracker. The goal of every game is the same: be the first to collect 20 lore.

Learning with a friend or family: get a 2-Player Starter Set. Each box has two 60-card decks, two lore trackers, 13 paper damage tokens and a rulebook. The first edition came out with Wilds Unknown on May 15, 2026 (Amber/Emerald and Amethyst/Ruby, MSRP $29.99). The newest, released with Hyperia City in October 2026, pairs "Technically Terrific" (Emerald/Sapphire, led by Baymax) with "Madcap Whimsy" (Amethyst/Steel, led by Madam Mim).

Never played a trading card game: start with Gateway. It's a guided learn-to-play box with two 30-card starting decks, four reward packs, a game board and a guidebook that teaches the rules step by step. It launched on August 9, 2024 at $24.99.

Playing on your own: pick one of the last single starter decks, from Fabled (2025) or Whispers in the Well (November 2025). They are the only single decks whose sets are still legal in Core Constructed, the main tournament format. Choose the characters you like most. You'll play the deck more.

Only want to collect: a Collection Starter Set ($29.99) has four booster packs, a foil promo card and a card portfolio, but no deck. The current Rapunzel Edition comes with Attack of the Vine! packs.

A note on rotation

Once a year, Disney Lorcana's Core Constructed format drops its four oldest sets. Since Attack of the Vine! (July 2026), Core decks can only use cards from Fabled onward, plus older cards that were reprinted in Fabled or later. Older starter decks are still fine for casual games at home, and every card stays legal in the Infinity format, apart from banned cards.

How a Lorcana starter deck works

Every Disney Lorcana deck follows the same deck-building rules, so a starter deck is also a template for your first custom deck:

  • At least 60 cards, and no more than four copies of any card with the same full name.
  • One or two inks out of six: Amber, Amethyst, Emerald, Ruby, Sapphire and Steel.
  • First to 20 lore wins. Your characters "quest" to collect lore, or challenge the other player's characters.

The easiest first upgrade is to buy a second copy of the same starter deck, or a few booster packs of the same set, and swap in more copies of the cards you like.

Quick answers

How many Disney Lorcana starter decks are there? 21 single starter decks from The First Chapter to Whispers in the Well, plus four more decks in the two 2-Player Starter Sets released in 2026.

What's the best Lorcana starter deck for beginners? For two people, the newest 2-Player Starter Set. For a complete beginner or a child, Gateway. For one player, a Fabled or Whispers in the Well deck.

How much is a Disney Lorcana starter deck? The first single starter decks launched at US $16.99. The first 2-Player Starter Set and the Collection Starter Sets are $29.99, and Gateway was $24.99 at launch. Store prices vary.

Are Lorcana starter decks tournament legal? In Core Constructed, only cards from Fabled onward (plus reprints) are legal since July 2026, so decks from Fabled, Whispers in the Well and the 2-Player Starter Sets work. Every older deck is still legal in Infinity.

Did Lorcana stop making starter decks? Single starter decks stopped after Whispers in the Well. Since 2026, new players get 2-Player Starter Sets with two decks in one box.

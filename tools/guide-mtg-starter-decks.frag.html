What is a Magic starter deck?

A Magic: The Gathering starter deck is a ready-to-play deck you can open and play straight away, with no deck building. Magic has more kinds of them than most card games, because it has more ways to play: 60-card decks for one-on-one games, 100-card Commander decks for groups of four, and 20-card Jumpstart half-decks that you shuffle together into a 40-card deck.

Wizards of the Coast's current beginner line-up has four parts: Beginner Boxes that teach the game step by step, Starter Kits with two 60-card decks, the new 60-Card Theme Decks that started in January 2026, and Starter Commander Decks for the most popular casual format. Stores that run Magic events also hand out free Welcome Decks.

Which Magic starter deck should you start with?

Never played, and learning with someone: get a Beginner Box. It has 10 themed Jumpstart half-decks of 20 cards, including two tutorial decks you play first, plus two gameboard playmats, two How to Play guides and two spindown life counters. The Foundations Beginner Box (November 2024) has an MSRP of $29.99. There are also Universes Beyond versions for Avatar: The Last Airbender (November 21, 2025) and Marvel Super Heroes (2026), at $34.99.

Never played, on your own: ask your local game store for a free Welcome Deck. Since Secrets of Strixhaven (April 2026) they are 40-card, single-color decks. You can also learn for free in MTG Arena, Magic's digital version.

Know the basics and want a two-player box: a Starter Kit has two 60-card decks, a how-to-play guide, reference cards and an MTG Arena code, with an MSRP of $19.99. The newest is the Final Fantasy Starter Kit (blue-black and red-white decks).

Want to play 60-card Standard at a store: pick a 60-Card Theme Deck. Each one is a Standard-legal 60-card deck with 5 double-sided tokens, a reference card and a strategy insert, built to stay legal for at least a year. The Lorwyn Eclipsed decks were $24.99 at US retail. Buying one in a store also unlocks the digital copy in MTG Arena.

Want to play Commander with friends: get a Starter Commander Deck. The newest five, the Foundations Starter Commander Decks (reported for October 2026), each have a 100-card deck with a traditional foil mythic rare, 10 double-sided tokens and a deck box. Their commanders are Giada (Calling All Angels), Sai (Keen Engineering), Ghoulcaller Gisa (Wretched Ranks), Lathliss (Reign of Dragons) and Ghalta (Tramplesaurus Rex).

Theme Decks: the newest starter line

60-Card Theme Decks launched with Lorwyn Eclipsed on January 23, 2026: Pirates, a blue-red deck of pirates and vehicles, and Angels, a green-white lifegain deck with two copies of Lyra Dawnbringer. Secrets of Strixhaven added Eerie (white-blue) and Lifegain (white-black) on April 24, 2026. Wizards says more themes will follow with future sets, and each deck comes with a QR code to an upgrade guide.

How a Magic deck works

Every starter deck follows the same deck-building rules, so it's also a template for your first custom deck:

  • 60-card decks: at least 60 cards and no more than four copies of any card, apart from basic lands. Each player starts at 20 life.
  • Commander decks: exactly 100 cards, only one copy of each card apart from basic lands, led by a legendary creature, your commander. Each player starts at 40 life.
  • Jumpstart: shuffle two 20-card half-decks together into a 40-card deck and play.
  • Five colors: white, blue, black, red and green. Most starter decks use one or two.

A note on Standard

Standard, the main 60-card format, only uses recent sets, so older cards rotate out over time. Theme Decks are designed to stay Standard-legal for at least a year after release. Commander and Jumpstart decks never rotate: every card stays legal in Commander unless it is banned.
